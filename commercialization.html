Commercialization

Research from the lab has twice left the bench and become a company.

C-SATS, Inc.

Seattle, WA · Founded 2014 · Acquired by Johnson & Johnson, 2018

C-SATS grew out of research on crowd-sourced assessment of technical skill: video of a surgeon's actual cases is assessed at scale, and the feedback returned anonymously so surgeons can see how their technique compares. Co-founded by Dr. Kowalewski, the company was acquired by Johnson & Johnson in 2018 and folded into its surgeon education and training platform.

Visit C-SATS

Lightside Surgical

Seattle, WA · Co-founded by Dr. Kowalewski, Chief Technical Officer

Lightside Surgical works on bringing surgical expertise to the point of injury, using simulation science and telerobotics to connect frontline medical providers with remote surgeons in real time — so a medic can stabilise a critically injured patient during evacuation.
