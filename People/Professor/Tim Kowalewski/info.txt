Bio:
Dr. Kowalewski directs the Medical Robotics and Devices Lab in the Department
of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Minnesota, where he has been on
the faculty since 2012 — first as the Richard and Barbara Nelson Assistant
Professor, and from 2020 as Associate Professor.

He holds a joint appointment in Electrical Engineering. His research advances
the emerging field of computational surgery, and centers
on four threads: instrumented surgical tools that make operating-room technique
quantitative; custom surgical robots that automate patient access and extract
procedural information; surgical simulation using virtual reality and realistic
tissues; and the Surgical Genome Project, a collaborative database of surgical
procedures with tool-tracking and sensor data.

He holds a Ph.D. (2012), M.S. (2009), and B.S. (2003) in Electrical Engineering
from the University of Washington, Seattle, where his doctoral work in the
Biorobotics Lab addressed quantifying surgical skill. He was a research
scientist on DARPA's "Trauma Pod: Operating Room of the Future" program, and
co-founded CSATS Inc., acquired by Johnson & Johnson in 2018. He is a recipient
of an NSF CAREER award and a McKnight Presidential Fellow.
