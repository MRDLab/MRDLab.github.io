Prospective students

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students and from undergraduates looking for hands-on research experience. When you write, it helps to include:

  • Your CV or resume
  • Which of our projects interests you, and why
  • Your background in robotics, controls, ME, or CS
  • The term you would like to start

Visiting & parking
